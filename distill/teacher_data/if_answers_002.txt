<<Two Ways to See the Water Cycle>>
One perspective is the physical view: the water cycle is a closed loop where the sun drives evaporation, vapour cools into clouds and precipitation returns water to land and sea.

A second perspective is the human view: the cycle is also a supply system that fills reservoirs, feeds farms and decides whether a town has enough drinking water in summer.

The physical view stresses the steady process and the conservation of water, while the human view stresses timing and place, because rain in the wrong month or location can mean flood or drought.

Seen together, the two perspectives show that the cycle is both natural law and daily concern, and good planning needs both.
